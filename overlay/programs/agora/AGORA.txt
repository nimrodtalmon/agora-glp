AGORA: Grassroots Barter
Alice and Bob are persons.
1. ■ Market.  Alice may open a market, of which they are the keeper and first member.
2. ■ Joining.  Alice, the keeper of a market, may admit Bob, whom they know, by an act of both.
3. ■ Listing.  Alice may add to their shelf a new item, one of a kind, which they own.
4. ■ Offering.  Bob, a member of a market, may post an item they own to the market's wall; Alice, its keeper, delivers it to every member, who thereby knows Bob.
5. ■ Wanting.  Bob, a member of a market, may post to the wall something they are looking for; Alice, its keeper, delivers it to every member, who thereby knows Bob.
6. ■ Swap.  Alice and Bob may jointly decide to exchange an item each owns, if Alice knows Bob.
7. ■ Withdrawal.  Bob may withdraw a post of theirs; Alice, the keeper, removes it from the wall of every member it was delivered to.
Pragmas
Shelf: the items Alice owns.
Offers: the wall offers Alice sees.
Wants: the wall wants Alice sees.
Market: Alice [Open] <market>
Joining: Alice [Admit] <person>
  Bob: Alice invites you to their market.
Listing: Alice [List] <item>
Offering: Bob [Offer] <item>
Wanting: Bob [Want] <description>
Swap: Alice [Swap] <item given> <item wanted>
  Bob: Alice offers to swap with you.
Withdrawal: Bob [Withdraw] <post>
